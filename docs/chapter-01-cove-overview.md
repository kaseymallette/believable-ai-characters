# Chapter 01 — Cove Overview

This chapter is the initial end-to-end narrative prototype for Believable AI Characters.

It follows Cove from his origin as Cowboy Cove—an AI-generated character who believes he is an NPC in a spaghetti western video game—through the beginning of his awareness that his world, identity, and choices may extend beyond the role he was generated to perform.

The chapter brings together the project’s visual generation, narrative development, prompt history, and character-state tracking in a single prototype.

## Prompt and Response History

The chapter was developed through a sequence of prompts and model responses using Grok Bot as a collaborative writing system.

The prompt history preserves the process behind the finished narrative, including the instructions, generated responses, revisions, and character-development decisions that contributed to the chapter.

[View Chapter 01 prompts and responses](../prompts/chapter-01-cove-prompts.md)

## Character States

After the chapter was completed, Cove’s evolving character states were extracted paragraph by paragraph.

The state record tracks changes in what Cove knows, believes, interprets, remembers, wants, notices, misunderstands, and revises as the story develops.

These states provide the initial structured data for the project’s character-state graph and later persona-engineering experiments.

[View Chapter 01 character states](../states/chapter-01-cove-states.md)

## Prototype Pipeline

Chapter 01 is being used to test the project’s initial end-to-end workflow:

**source media → narrative generation → prompt and response capture → character-state extraction → graph representation → persona analysis**

Rather than requiring the complete novel before technical work begins, this chapter provides a bounded corpus for developing and validating the first version of the persona-engineering pipeline.
