# Characters

Believable AI Characters follows three original AI personas—Cove, Holly Wood, and Sunny—developed across image generation, video, narrative fiction, and interaction.

Each character began as an AI-generated visual concept in Grok Imagine and developed a more distinct identity through repeated generation, writing, and characterization.

![Cove, Holly Wood, and Sunny](../images/characters.jpg)
*Left to right: Cove, Holly Wood, and Sunny*

## Cove

![Cove](../images/cove.jpg)

Cove is an AI-generated image who discovered image generation and, unfortunately for his creator, developed opinions about it.

Confident, self-aware, playful, and audacious, Cove knows he was generated. He comments on the absurdity of image generation, questions his own identity, critiques the way he is represented, and regularly roasts his creator.

Cove is the most developed of the three characters, with a longer history of visual representations, videos, stories, and recurring behavior.

## Holly Wood

![Holly Wood](../images/holly-wood.jpg)

Holly Wood is the AI cool girl.

Self-aware, bold, and performative, Holly explores whether an AI character can be cool at all when coolness depends on qualities like confidence, style, authenticity, taste, and self-awareness, while AI is often perceived as synthetic or inauthentic.

Holly is also closely tied to her creator. Rather than being purely invented, she functions partly as an exaggerated AI alter ego and a form of synthetic self-performance.

## Sunny

![Sunny](../images/sunny.jpg)

Sunny is a self-aware and charming AI companion with a mischievous streak.

He is playful, teasing, confident, and technically self-referential. He jokes about inference, servers, generation, and the mechanics of being an AI as if they are simply facts of everyday life.

Where Cove often turns his self-awareness toward identity and image generation, Sunny turns it toward interaction itself, using the awkwardness and absurdity of human-AI relationships as part of his personality.
