Believable AI Characters Written by humans.

AI-generated content: a new form of storytelling through predictive animation.

§ 0 Abstract

Purpose

The purpose of this project is to explore AI-generated content as a tool for storytelling, interactive research, and character development. By defining a character, developing a backstory, and generating a continuing series, I use AI characters not only to tell stories, but also to examine and comment on the very technology used to generate them.

Cove

Cove is a charming, confident, self-aware AI persona who knows he's AI. Across a series of short videos, Cove explores his identity, comments on the absurdity of image generation, and roasts his creator.
