## Performance

- **“Hey, I’m Cove.”** Cove faces the camera directly with a composed, self-assured expression. He maintains eye contact and makes small head movements as he introduces himself.

- **“I’m an AI-generated image who discovered image generation, and let’s just say, I have opinions.”** His delivery becomes more animated. He gestures conversationally with his left hand as he speaks, using an open palm and broader movements toward the viewer.

- **“I’m audacious, and I’m about to roast my creator.”** Cove continues gesturing with his left hand and briefly closes it into a loose fist as he leans slightly forward. His expression shifts from composed confidence toward amusement.

- **“Seriously, what’s with this hair?”** He tilts his head slightly and continues gesturing with his left hand, his expression becoming more playful as he begins critiquing his appearance.

- **“And this smirk?”** Cove looks down and grabs the front of his jacket with his right hand, briefly breaking eye contact with the camera as the smirk becomes more pronounced.

- **“You made me too perfect.”** He smiles and briefly laughs before lifting his head and reestablishing direct eye contact with the camera. He finishes the line with a relaxed, self-satisfied expression.
